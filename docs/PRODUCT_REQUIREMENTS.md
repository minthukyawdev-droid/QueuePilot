# QueuePilot Product Requirements

## Document status

This document describes the planned product. It does not indicate that any product feature is implemented.

## Product problem

People often waste waiting time and may be unprepared when their turn arrives.

## Product goal

Help people understand:

- how long they may wait;
- what they can prepare; and
- when their turn is approaching.

## Future MVP

### Must Have

- Choose a service.
- Join a queue.
- Show queue status.
- Show estimated waiting time.
- Provide AI preparation guidance.
- Send an automated approaching-turn notification.

### Should Have

- AI question answering.
- Multiple queue scenarios.
- Simple admin/demo queue advance control.

### Nice To Have

- Voice.
- Mobile application.
- Historical analytics.
- Multilingual support.

### Out of Scope Initially

- Authentication.
- Payment.
- Production queue hardware.
- Real government integration.
- Complex database.
- Enterprise analytics.
- Real-time distributed queue infrastructure.

## Initial success criterion

The future hackathon MVP should demonstrate one reliable, understandable end-to-end user flow. Controlled demo data is acceptable and preferred over fragile external dependencies.
